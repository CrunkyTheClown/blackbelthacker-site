FAQ

Do you need access to production?

Sometimes. External tests often need no credentials. Authenticated and internal tests need access you control, shared after the NDA, and only for the systems in the signed scope.

How do you avoid breaking things?

Testing stays inside the signed windows. I do not run denial of service. Destructive checks stay out unless the scope includes them. On production I stay on the rate and the paths we agreed.

Who sees the report?

You, and the people you name. I do not publish findings from an engagement. The report is encrypted, and the data is destroyed after the retention period in the contract.

Do you test cloud hosted systems?

Yes, within the cloud provider's testing policy. You confirm that policy before testing starts.

Can you test AI agents we built?

Yes. The Brown Belt tier covers agents and LLM applications you own, or that you are authorized to have tested.

How is payment handled?

A deposit is invoiced through Stripe. The balance is invoiced when the report is delivered.
